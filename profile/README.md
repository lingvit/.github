### What it is

Lingvit adds multilingual support to websites, web apps and SaaS products. Anything that runs in a browser can be translated — without forking your codebase or rebuilding your content pipeline.

### From zero to multilingual

1. **Add the script** — one tag on your site.
2. **Choose languages** — 30+ available.
3. **Ship** — translations are generated automatically and served to your visitors.

### Why developers pick it

- **No refactor.** Your markup stays yours; localization sits on top.
- **Automatic first, human-reviewable after.** Start with machine translation, refine where it matters.
- **Predictable pricing.** Word-based subscription plans, so cost follows the amount of text you translate.
- **Broad reach.** Plugins, an SDK and integrations for the stacks people actually run.
